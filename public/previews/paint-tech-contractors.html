The Paint Tech 5-Stage Preparation Standard

Every room goes through five documented stages before we pull the masking tape.

Stage 01

Complete Containment & Taping

Hardwood floors, countertops, hardware, and trim lines are masked with precision painter's tape and rosin paper.

Stage 02

Drywall Cutting, Patching & Caulking

Clean cutouts around outlets, nail-pop repair, seam taping, and elastomeric caulk along all trim gaps.

Common Questions Before We Start

How do you prevent flashing spots where drywall was patched? +

We sand every patch flush and seal bare drywall compound with a dedicated high-build primer before rolling the two finish coats.

Do you repair drywall and caulk trim gaps as part of painting? +

Yes. Every interior job includes nail-hole filling, crack patching, and caulking gaps along baseboards and door casings.
