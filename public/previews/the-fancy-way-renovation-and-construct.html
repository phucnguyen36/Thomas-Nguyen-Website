Common Illinois Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

How deep do you set fence and deck posts? +

Posts are set 24 to 36 inches deep in concrete footings with gravel base drainage so gates stay level and posts don't lean.

Do you tear down and haul off our old rotten fence or deck? +

Yes. Full demolition and haul-off can be included right in your written quote.

Do you use nails or structural screws on deck boards and rails? +

We use exterior coated structural screws and galvanized Simpson Strong-Tie joist hangers throughout.

Is the yard measurement free? +

Yes. We measure your property lines and elevation and send a clear written bid within 24 hours.
