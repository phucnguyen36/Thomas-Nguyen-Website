Common Washington Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

Do you bring your own generator and welder to my property? +

Yes. Our mobile rig carries self-contained welding power, grinders, and torches so we work anywhere without plugging into your house.

For junk removal, do I have to pile everything at the curb? +

No. We remove items directly from garages, backyards, sheds, or upstairs rooms and sweep clean afterward.

Can you weld custom gate latches or repair broken trailer axles? +

Yes. Send a quick description in the form below and we will confirm specs and turnaround right away.

Are dump fees included in hauling quotes? +

Yes. Every hauling bid includes labor, fuel, and landfill/recycling disposal fees.
