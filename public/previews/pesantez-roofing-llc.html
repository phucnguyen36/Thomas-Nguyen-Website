Common Rhode Island Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

How long does a full residential roof replacement take? +

Most average-sized shingle roofs are torn off, dry-in sealed, shingled, and magnet-swept in 1 to 2 days.

What happens if you find rotted decking plywood after tear-off? +

We photograph any soft decking immediately, show you before replacing it, and swap sheets at a transparent per-sheet rate.

Do you clean up all nails from the driveway and lawn? +

Yes. We tarp landscaping before tear-off and run rolling magnetic nail sweeps across the driveway, yard, and flower beds.

Is the roof inspection and written estimate free? +

Yes. We inspect flashing, valleys, and decking and hand you a clear written quote within 24 hours.
