Why Bibian Epoxy Floors Never Peel Under Texas Heat

Our 4-stage mechanical diamond-grinding and polyaspartic sealing protocol.

Direct Line: (832) 691-3190
Stage 01

Mechanical Diamond Grinding

We never use weak acid washes. We diamond-grind the concrete slab to open the pores for a permanent mechanical bond.

Stage 02

Crack & Joint Remediation

All expansion joints, spalls, and divots are chased and filled flush with high-strength structural filler.

Stage 03

Moisture-Vapor Primer & 100%Broadcast

Deep-wicking epoxy base coat followed by a full 100% broadcast of decorative vinyl flake.

Stage 04

UV-Stable Polyaspartic Clear Topcoat

Chemical-resistant, hot-tire-proof topcoat that will not yellow or lift in Houston humidity.

Common Questions Before We Start

Why do cheap DIY garage epoxy kits peel within a year? +

Hardware store kits use water-based paint over unground concrete. We mechanically diamond-grind the slab so our industrial base coat locks directly into the concrete pores.

Can you handle large 3,000+ sq.ft. residential or commercial floors? +

Yes. As shown in our 3,800 sq.ft. Chappell Hill project, we have the equipment and crew for expansive custom homes, warehouses, and shops.
