Common North Carolina Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

Do you move furniture and protect floors before painting? +

Yes. We cover all flooring with rosin paper or canvas drop cloths, mask windows and fixtures, and carefully move heavy furniture away from walls.

What paint brands and finishes do you use? +

We use top-tier Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore architectural lines matched to each surface, including washable matte for living areas and satin/semi-gloss for trim.

How long does an average exterior or interior project take? +

Most 3-bedroom interiors or single-story exteriors finish in 2 to 4 days depending on trim detail and drying conditions.

Is the color walk-through and written estimate free? +

Yes. We inspect every wall or siding section in person and give you a fixed written bid within 24 hours.
