Common Massachusetts Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

Can you safely remove a dead tree leaning over my roof or fence? +

Yes. We use sectional rigging ropes and lowering devices to control every limb safely away from shingles, gutters, and fences.

Do you haul away the logs and wood chips? +

Yes. You can choose full brush and log haul-off or keep clean mulch chips for garden beds.

How deep do you grind tree stumps? +

We grind stumps 6 to 8 inches below grade including major surface roots so grass or driveways can go right over the spot.
