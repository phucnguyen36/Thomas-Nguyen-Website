How Superior Concrete Decor LLC Builds Slabs That Resist Cracking

Every pour follows a 4-stage structural specification from excavation to final curing.

Direct Line: (863) 441-0894
STAGE 01

Subgrade Excavation & Compaction

We excavate organic topsoil down to stable native clay or soil and compact the trench before any aggregate is placed.

STAGE 02

4 to 6 Inch Crushed Stone Base

Compacted road-grade crushed gravel provides uniform load distribution and prevents frost heave under your slab.

STAGE 03

Steel Rebar & 4,000+ PSI Pour

Reinforced with steel rebar or wire mesh and poured with high-strength ready-mix concrete.

STAGE 04

Control Joints & Penetrating Sealer

Toohey or saw-cut expansion joints are placed at strict intervals and sealed for multi-decade durability.
