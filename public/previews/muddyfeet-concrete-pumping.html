Common Texas Project Questions

Straight answers on prep, timelines, haul-off, and pricing before we step onto your property.

How thick should my driveway be for pickup trucks or an RV? +

Standard passenger driveways are poured at 4 inches with 4,000 PSI concrete and #4 rebar. For heavy diesel trucks, boat trailers, or RV pads, we recommend 5 to 6 inches with a thickened perimeter edge.

Do you tear out and haul off my old cracked concrete? +

Yes. We handle demolition, haul-off, re-grading, and crushed base compaction so your new slab sits on solid ground.

How soon can we walk and park on the new pour? +

Foot traffic is safe after 24 to 48 hours. Passenger vehicles can park after 7 days; heavy trucks or RVs should wait 14 to 28 days for full cure strength.

Is the on-site measurement really free with no hidden fees? +

Yes. We measure your site at zero cost and deliver an itemized written bid with zero surprise fuel or pump surcharges.
